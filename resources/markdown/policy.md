This Privacy Policy explains how **BluePin Inc** ("BluePin", "we", "us", "our"), the company responsible for Dot.Engage, collects, uses, stores and shares personal information when you use Dot.Engage, whether you are a member of a business that uses it or someone invited to take part in an engagement. It is written to align with South Africa's **Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA")**.

## 1. Who we are

BluePin Inc is the responsible party for the personal information Dot.Engage processes. Our Information Officer can be reached at [privacy@infodot.co.za](mailto:privacy@infodot.co.za) for any question, request or concern about your personal information. The business that creates an engagement decides which documents are put in it and who is invited; it is responsible for having the right to share them.

## 2. What we collect

- **Account information** for members of a business: name, email address and the details your Dot Ecosystem account provides. Passwords are never stored by Dot.Engage in plain text.
- **Invitation details** for people invited from outside a business: the name and email address the inviting person entered, the role they were given, and when the invitation was opened.
- **One-time codes**: when you confirm your email address we store a keyed hash of the code, never the code, for a few minutes.
- **Engagement content**: documents, document versions, notes, proposed changes, approvals, discussion messages and meeting details that participants add.
- **Signing evidence**: the signature you draw, type or upload, the sentence you agreed to, the time, your network address, your browser and device description, and how your identity was confirmed.
- **History**: a record of what each participant did in an engagement (for example opened, commented, signed, joined a meeting), with time and, for participants, network address and browser.
- **Meetings**: who was admitted, when they joined and left, and whether their microphone, camera and screen sharing were on. The audio and video travel between participants' browsers; Dot.Engage does not record or transcribe them. Short technical messages used to set up the connection are deleted within an hour.
- **Technical information**: IP address, browser and device information and session activity, collected automatically for security and to keep you signed in.

## 3. Why we process your information

- to create and maintain accounts and authenticate people (InfoDot sign-in for members, an emailed code for invited people);
- to let businesses discuss, change, approve and sign agreements together;
- to produce evidence of who signed what and when, so a signed agreement can be checked later;
- to send notices about an engagement (invitations, requests to sign, meetings, changes); emails name the engagement and what happened but never include document text; and
- to keep Dot.Engage secure and prevent abuse.

## 4. Who sees what

An invited person sees only the engagement they were invited to and only the documents shared with them. Members of the owning business see everything in their business's engagements. We do not sell personal information and we do not use it for advertising. Email is sent through the mail provider configured for the platform. If a deployment is set up to timestamp agreements with an independent authority, only the SHA-256 fingerprint of the evidence record is sent to it, never names or content.

## 5. Ecosystem single sign-on

When you sign in to Dot.Engage through InfoDot, a short-lived, single-use token confirms who you are without exposing your password to the connected platform.

## 6. Cookies

Dot.Engage uses a small number of strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in and to protect forms from cross-site attacks. See our [Cookie Policy](/cookies).

## 7. How long we keep your information

Account and engagement data are kept while the business keeps them. Signed agreements, their certificate, the evidence record and the history are kept for at least seven years because they may be needed as evidence, and a business that holds signed agreements cannot be deleted inside that period. Invitation links expire after 14 days unless a signing request or a completed agreement extends them, and can be revoked by the inviting business at any time. One-time codes expire within minutes.

## 8. How we protect your information

Documents are stored on private disks and served only to people allowed to see them. Invitation links are stored as hashes. Every action is written to a history that is chained so that tampering shows. Signing evidence is sealed so that changes are detectable. We apply reasonable technical and organisational security measures appropriate to the information we hold.

## 9. Your rights under POPIA

You have the right to:

- ask us to confirm what personal information we hold about you, and to access it;
- ask us to correct or delete personal information that is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, misleading or obtained unlawfully;
- object, on reasonable grounds, to the processing of your personal information;
- withdraw any consent you gave, for a specific purpose;
- ask us not to use your information for direct marketing; and
- complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at [inforegulator.org.za](https://inforegulator.org.za) if you believe we processed your information unlawfully.

To exercise these rights, contact our Information Officer at [privacy@infodot.co.za](mailto:privacy@infodot.co.za). Deleting a signed agreement, a signature or its evidence may not be possible where it forms evidence of an agreement between parties.

## 10. Children

Dot.Engage is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children without the consent of a parent or guardian as required by POPIA.

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will change the "Last updated" date when we do, and for material changes we will take reasonable steps to let you know before they take effect.
